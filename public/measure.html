Measure and report

Your measurement plan now. At 90 days, add the numbers and it becomes your report.

Why?

Completion rates don't show whether anyone works differently. This follows four simple questions, from "did it help?" to "does it matter?" (the Kirkpatrick levels), using team totals only.

Enter numbers at the start and at 90 days. Leave blank what you didn't measure.
