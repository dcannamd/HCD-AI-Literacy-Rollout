AI use guide

Your AI rules on one page: approved tools, red lines and what to do if something goes wrong.

Why?

People can't follow rules they can't find or understand. One short page, shared before training starts, answers the questions everyone has: which tools, what's off-limits, and is it safe to admit a mistake.

A starting point, not legal advice. Have your final guide reviewed by someone who knows employment and privacy law where you operate.
