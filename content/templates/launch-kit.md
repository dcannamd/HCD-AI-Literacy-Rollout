## 2. Talking points for managers

**Three things to say**

- "This is about building skills, for everyone, at your own pace."
- "Your learning plan is yours. I won't see it unless you share it."
- "Nobody gets in trouble for telling us how they already use AI."

**Questions you'll hear**

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Will AI replace my job? | This program is about skills. We'll be open about how AI changes our work, and you'll have a say. |
| Will you track what I do? | No. {{data_answer}} |
| I already use AI. Am I in trouble? | No. From now on, use approved tools{{#if tools}}: {{tools}}{{/if}}. |
| I'm not good with tech. | Sessions are short and start with the basics. Mistakes are part of learning. |
| How much time will it take? | About 20 minutes a week{{#if work_time}}, during work time{{/if}}. |
